Coverage & sources

Geographic geometry is retained as vectors and tiled in the browser. Labels are rendered at the current screen resolution, so they stay sharp as you zoom. This map includes the assembled nationwide inventories; literal 100% completeness is not certified. It does not contain live running information or journey planning.

  • OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL — Geofabrik Japan extract, 24 October 2026. Passenger-positive track selection excludes freight-only lines and yards. Public ropeways are curated; ski-only lifts are excluded.
  • MLIT national rail inventory (2022) — cross-check and long-term suspension context. It is not treated as a current opening/closure list.
  • kametaro7 / japan-ferry-map — 13 September 2026 water-service inventory, credited ODbL geometry, supplemented with selected operator updates and the MLIT scheduled passenger-operator directory.
  • Short ferry dashes indicate approximate, partial or direct connections. A hollow diamond marks a sightseeing departure point with no published fixed circuit. Grey suspension layers are off by default.
  • Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (地理院タイル) — optional aerial imagery, including satellite mosaics at smaller scales. Background tiles require an internet connection; transport geometry and search are embedded in this file.
  • OpenStreetMap standard tiles — optional roads, buildings and place labels. Tiles are requested for the current view and cached by the browser.
  • MapLibre GL JS 6.15.0 — BSD-3-Clause renderer. The application and map data run locally; no account or API key is needed.

Historical railways combines recorded eras, including former industrial and forest lines. Brown dashes show lines; hollow circles show former stations and squares show sites. Dates and geometry quality are given in each feature’s details. Minami-Daitō paths are schematic with 1 km display uncertainty. Miyako’s sugar railway and Kitadaitō’s phosphate tramway are represented by documented sites where full alignments are unavailable. See the historical coverage and source notes.

At high zoom, all mapped station labels can be displayed. Labels at the same interchange may overlap; use search or click the station to isolate a name. Ferry courses are indicative transport connections, not navigation charts.
